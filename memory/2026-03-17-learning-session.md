## Key Lessons (What Changed Thinking)

### Lesson 1: Infrastructure Moat Is Stronger Than Feature Moat
- **Before:** Thought defensibility came from better UI/UX
- **After:** Defensibility comes from workflow lock-in (once embedded, switching = engineer death)
- **Action:** Design every feature with embedding in mind

### Lesson 2: Niche Domination > Broad Growth
- **Before:** "Get as many teams as possible"
- **After:** "Own 50%+ of crypto trading teams, even if that's only 30 teams"
- **Action:** Stop thinking about TAM expansion until current niche is saturated

### Lesson 3: Vanity Metrics Kill Startups
- **Before:** Would celebrate "50 signups!"
- **After:** Would celebrate "10 active teams, 8 retained this week, 1 paid"
- **Action:** Weekly review of DAU + retention + revenue only

### Lesson 4: Shipping Over Perfection Is Non-Negotiable
- **Before:** Thought 2-week cycles were aggressive
- **After:** Graham said startups that disappear for 2 months usually die; stay visible
- **Action:** Weekly shipping, no exceptions

### Lesson 5: Layer 3 (Infrastructure) Gets Higher Valuation Than Layer 4-5 (Applications)
- **Before:** Might build a trading app on top of Lyyte eventually
- **After:** Never build the app; let others do it; stay infrastructure
- **Action:** Every expansion idea is tested against: "Does this make us less defensible infrastructure?"
