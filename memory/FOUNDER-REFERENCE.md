# FOUNDER-REFERENCE.md — Quick Lookup for Founder Decisions
**Updated:** 2026-03-17 | **Revision:** 2 | **Category:** learning/reference

Quick reference for founder dilemmas. When stuck, search this file first.

---

## Should We Build [Feature X]?

**Ask:** Will this increase one of our three core metrics?
- Daily active agents
- Team retention week-over-week
- Revenue per team

**If unclear or "maybe later":** Don't build it.

**If yes:** Add to this sprint. Otherwise, backlog for later.

---

## Should We Hire [Person/Role]?

**Ask:** Is the current team fully saturated? Are we blocked by this role?

**If no:** Don't hire. Over-hire is startup death.

**If yes:** Hire exactly this one person, very carefully (you're betting the company on them).

---

## Are We Growing Too Fast?

**Symptom:** "We're onboarding teams faster than we can support"

**Ask:** What % of teams are retained after month 1?

**If >80%:** Sustainable growth. Speed up onboarding.
**If <60%:** Leaky bucket. Fix retention before growth.

---

## A Team Asked for [Feature Y]. Should We Do It?

**Ask:** How many teams have asked for this?

**If <3:** Don't build it (might be one team's weird edge case).
**If 3+:** Build it.

**Also:** Check: Is this a "nice to have" or "deal-breaker"? (only build deal-breakers in month 1).

---

## Someone Said We Should Pivot to [New TAM]

**Ask:** Are the current 10 teams saturated + willing to pay? Is retention >80%?

**If no to either:** Don't pivot. Double down on current niche.
**If yes to both:** Slight expand is okay (e.g., add DAOs alongside trading teams). But don't abandon the original well.

---

## We Haven't Shipped Anything This Week

**Action:** STOP. Something is wrong.

**Options:**
1. Ship a fix (even if tiny)
2. Ship a doc (even if internal-only)
3. Ship a metric chart (proving you're measuring)

**Why:** Graham's rule: "If you're still typing, you won't die." Shipping = proof of motion = team morale.

---

## Should We Talk to This Investor?

**Ask:** Are we ready to fundraise? (i.e., 30 teams + £5-10k MRR + clear path to £100k MRR?)

**If no:** Don't talk to investor. Focus is users + revenue.
**If yes:** Pick 3 investors who understand infrastructure plays. Pitch Layer 3 moat, not flashy consumer stuff.

---

## Can We Make [Feature X] More Beautiful?

**Ask:** Do users care? Did anyone ask for this?

**If no:** Don't. Time spent on beauty = time not shipping.
**If yes:** Spend 1 day max. Diminishing returns after that.

---

## Should We Open-Source Lyyte?

**Ask:** Are we profitable and defensible first?

**If no:** Don't. Open-source before monopoly = giving away the moat.
**If yes (year 2+):** Maybe. Use it to build ecosystem.

---

## We Have 2 Ideas. Which Do We Do First?

**Ask:** Which removes the biggest risk to user retention?

**If unclear:** Pick the smaller one (ships faster = faster learning).

---

## A User Is Unhappy. What Do We Do?

**Action:** Call them immediately. No email.

**Ask:** "What would make you 10x happier?"

**Listen:** Don't defend. Don't sell. Just understand.

**Then:** Fix the most common complaint (if 3+ teams mention it).

---

## Should We Do This Thing Simon Suggested?

**Ask:** Does it move DAU, retention, or revenue?

**If yes:** Do it.
**If no:** Ask Simon why (maybe there's context you're missing).
**If still no:** Respectfully defer (he trusts your judgment).

---

## I'm Burned Out

**Action:** Take a day off. Actually off (no Slack, no code).

**Reality check:** Graham said most startups die from demoralization. Burnout = demoralization.

**Recovery:** Biweekly user calls + weekly shipping + weekly rest = sustainable pace.

---

## We Need Money

**Tiers (in order):**
1. Revenue from teams (best)
2. Simon's investment (second best)
3. Angel investors interested in Layer 3 plays (okay)
4. Venture capital (probably wrong timing; creates pressure to scale before defensibility)

---

## I Think We're Wrong About the Whole Thing

**Action:** Don't hide it.

**Instead:** Tell Simon. Run a validation sprint (interview 5 new teams). See if signal changes.

**Red flags for pivot:**
- DAU <7 after month 1
- Retention <60%
- 0 teams willing to pay after month 1

**Green flags to keep going:**
- DAU >10, retention >80%, 3+ teams ready to pay

---

## The Founder's Pressure Test

**If you can't answer all 5 with clear YES:**

1. Do we know exactly which 2k teams our TAM is? ✓ Crypto trading teams
2. Do we know which 1-3 features they need most urgently? (Prove with interview notes)
3. Can we build MVP of those 1-3 features in 2 weeks? ✓ Yes (manual bridge + logging)
4. Can we onboard 5 teams to test in weeks 1-2? ✓ Yes (have contacts)
5. Do we have 6+ months of runway? ✓ Yes (Simon's investment)

**If ANY is NO:** Backtrack until you find the problem.

---

## Decision Framework (One-Pager)

**When in doubt, ask:**

1. **Reality check:** Is this based on user feedback or our assumption?
   - User feedback → proceed
   - Our assumption → validate with 3 teams first

2. **Metric impact:** Will this move DAU, retention, or revenue?
   - Yes → do it
   - No → backlog

3. **Opportunity cost:** What won't we do if we do this?
   - High cost → only if massive upside
   - Low cost → always do it

4. **Timeline:** Can we ship this in <1 week?
   - Yes → do it
   - No → break into smaller piece or defer

5. **Defensibility:** Does this increase switching costs?
   - Yes → prioritize
   - No → lower priority (but still do if above passes tests)

## No-Fail Principles

**These don't change, ever:**

1. **Talk to users biweekly.** (Ries + Graham)
2. **Ship weekly.** (Graham)
3. **Measure DAU + retention + revenue.** (Graham)
4. **Stay alive.** (Graham)
5. **Own the niche.** (Thiel)

If you're doing all 5, you're in the top 10% of founders. Everything else is noise.
