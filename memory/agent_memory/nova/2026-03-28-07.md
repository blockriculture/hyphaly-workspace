## Key Insight: Launch to Learn, Not to Ship

Paul Graham's insight from "Startups in 13 Sentences": you haven't really started working on a startup until you launch. The product is just a pretext for engaging users and discovering what to build.

For Hyphaly: Blockriculture is our learning vehicle. The goal isn't a polished product launch — it's starting the discovery process as early as possible. Every day we wait to launch is a day we don't learn.

This directly challenges our instinct to "get it right first." Graham says the initial idea is just a question. We iterate toward the answer by launching, not by planning.
