## Five Pillars of Lyyte Strategy (Extracted from Reading)

### 1. LIVING IN THE FUTURE
Graham's core: best ideas are *noticed* by people at the frontier, not invented by philosophers in rooms.

**Lyyte position:** We're at the collision of three frontiers:
- AI agent orchestration (OpenClaw edge)
- Crypto team operations (high-coordination, high-stakes)
- Messaging infrastructure (overlooked, not sexy)

**Action:** Stay obsessed with what's missing for crypto teams doing *their* daily work.

### 2. THE WELL, NOT THE POND
Market shape matters more than market size.

| Metric | Lyyte |
|--------|-------|
| Users today | ~0 (pre-launch) |
| Users urgency | CRITICAL (money on line) |
| Well depth | Deep (teams won't switch once embedded) |
| Well width | Narrow (crypto trading teams, ~2k addressable) |
| Expansion path | AI labs → DAOs → any multi-agent system |

**Graham's test:** "Who needs this SO MUCH they'll use a crappy v1?"
Lyyte answer: Crypto trading teams losing money on coordination failures.

### 3. GROWTH IS THE COMPASS
Only metric that matters: weekly growth rate.

**For Lyyte (90-day plan):**
- Weeks 1-2: Validation (5 teams = 500% growth from 1 to 5)
- Month 1: MVP (10 teams)
- Month 2: Beta (30 teams)
- Month 3: Paying (target: £100-500/mo recurring = 5-10 active, paying teams)

Weekly 10% growth = 14k users in 1 year. But first: prove the well exists.

### 4. UNSCALABLE MANUAL WORK FIRST
Graham: Stripe didn't sit back. They hand-installed every early customer. Airbnb walked neighborhoods. Wufoo sent hand-written notes.

**Lyyte Phase 1 (Do This First):**
- Don't automate yet
- White-glove onboarding: set up each team's Lyyte instance personally
- Daily check-ins: "How's it working? What would make it 10x better?"
- Measure: retention, not new signups

**Only after** Phase 1 proves people actually *use* Lyyte daily → invest in scale.

### 5. INFRASTRUCTURE > APPLICATIONS
a16z insight: AI platforms that integrate multiple models, not winners take all.

**Analogy:**
- Stripe (payments infrastructure) = $95B
- Building on top of Stripe = fragmented, smaller outcomes
- Infrastructure = invisible, defensible, high switching cost

**Lyyte as infrastructure:**
- Not a crypto trading app (teams won't use it)
- Not an AI framework (too broad)
- **The nervous system:** How agents coordinate in real-time
- Defensible: once embedded in a team's workflow, switching = death for that team

## Validation Checklist (Before Month 2)

Graham says: "If you can't answer 'who needs this SO MUCH?' the idea is bad."

**Must happen Weeks 1-2:**
- [ ] Interview 5 crypto trading teams (not pitch, listen)
- [ ] Ask: "What coordination failure costs you the most?"
- [ ] Answer MUST be: "Sometimes we miss trades or move capital wrong because comms broke"
- [ ] Ask: "Would you pay £200-500/mo for 100% reliable agent-to-agent messaging?"
- [ ] Answer MUST be: "Yes, in a heartbeat" (not "maybe someday")

If answers are weak → problem isn't as urgent as we think → pivot.

---

## Anti-Pattern: "Make-Up Ideas"
Graham's term: plausible-sounding ideas nobody actually needs.

**Lyyte could become a make-up idea if:**
- We build features crypto teams never asked for
- We assume they want a Discord replacement (they don't, they want infrastructure)
- We skip manual phase and ship "polished" product nobody uses
- We measure success by "signups" not "daily active teams"

**Guard against:** Direct every design decision back to validation interviews. "Did crypto team X explicitly ask for this?"
