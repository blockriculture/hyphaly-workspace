## [micro] Founder Mode: Manager Mode Destroys Companies

**Source:** Paul Graham, "Founder Mode" (September 2024)

**Insight:** Brian Chesky and Airbnb nearly collapsed when he followed conventional wisdom: "hire good people and give them room." This manager mode — treating subtrees of the org chart as black boxes — is wrong for founders. C-level execs are often expert at managing up and hiding problems. Founders get gaslit from both sides. The distress founders feel when advised to delegate everything is valid — it's a signal they're being pushed toward a mode that works against them.

**What this means for Lyyte/Hyphaly:** As we scale from 7 agents, Simon must resist the pull toward manager mode. The technical delegation to Linus is correct and necessary. But staying close to product, users, direction, and culture is equally non-negotiable. Delegating the "what" to Linus while staying deeply embedded in the "why" and "what for" IS founder mode. The moment Simon has to say "I delegated that" as explanation for a decision, something has gone wrong.
