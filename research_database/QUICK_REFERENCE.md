## ⏱️ Latency Breakdown (Why It Matters)

```
Signal created: 0ms
  ↓ (0.5-5ms)
Transmitted to bot: 5ms
  ↓ (50-100ms) ← BIGGEST LATENCY
Parsed & validated: 25ms
  ↓ (5-20ms)
Decision logic: 40ms
  ↓ (10-50ms)
Portfolio risk check: 90ms
  ↓ (20-100ms)
Order created: 105ms
  ↓ (1-5ms)
Exchange receives: 115ms
  ↓ (20-100ms)
Filled: 145-250ms total
```

**Industry average: 250ms | Best-in-class: 150ms | Blockriculture target: <100ms**

**Real impact:** At 100ms slower, arbitrage traders lose 50-70% of profits.
